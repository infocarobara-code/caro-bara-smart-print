neutral white and charcoal, warm oak surfaces, restrained cyan, magenta and
yellow print accents. Keep the existing Caro Bara logo and readable UI icons.

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. These are illustrative
service samples; they must not be described as actual Caro Bara premises,
completed client projects, or photographs of real employees.

with crop-safe margins, natural shadows and material textures; avoid CGI,

collages. Subjects: large-format printer, vinyl/acrylic/aluminum swatches,
paper stationery, illuminated sign samples, planning desk, frosted window

garments and heat press, rigid fabrication materials, identity presentation,
